# Voice-over script (ElevenLabs)

First person, as the filmmaker. About 100 words for 45 seconds.

## Settings

- Model: Eleven v3 (the tags in square brackets only work on v3).
- Voice: warm British male, 30s–40s, conversational, not an announcer.
- Stability: Natural. Speed: default.
- Generate it as one take so the tone stays consistent, then export MP3 or WAV.

## Paste this

```
[gently, curious] Think a wedding film means being told where to stand... all day?

[warm, reassuring] It doesn't. [pause] Your day just... happens.

[softly] The glance down the aisle. Your dad's speech. The last song.

[calm] I follow. Quietly. From the background.

[smiling] These are real couples, on real days. [pause] And your films arrive in a private gallery, in eight to ten weeks.

[light, clear] Booking is simple. Check your date. We have a relaxed call. A small fee holds your day. [warmly] And then... you relive it.

[inviting] Want more of the day on film? Six hours, ten, or the whole day with two filmmakers. From fourteen hundred and fifty pounds.

[softly, heartfelt] Films that feel the way the day did.

[warm] Check your date.
```
